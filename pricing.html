Frequently asked questions

How long does a takeoff take?

It depends on the size and complexity of the plan set. You can watch the status of every job in the app, and Rush is available when a bid deadline is close.

What counts as commercial?

Any commercial building, including a shingle roof on a commercial building. Houses and other residential buildings use residential pricing.

How do promo codes work?

Enter your code at checkout. It discounts the service level price only. Add-ons such as Rush or extra buildings are still charged.

Can I request revisions?

Yes. Additional revisions are $29 each.

Is the takeoff service a subscription?

No. Takeoffs are priced per project. SureSquare CRM will be a separate subscription.
