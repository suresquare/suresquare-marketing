Roofing takeoffs built for bidding

SureSquare is a roofing takeoff and estimating service that helps contractors bid with confidence. We turn drawings and specifications into clean, consistent quantities so you can price faster and protect your margin.

  • Steep-slope roofing takeoffs (shingle)
  • Low-slope and flat roofing takeoffs (TPO, EPDM, PVC)
  • Annotated plan markups
  • Quantity reports you can download as PDF or CSV

Experience

20 years of estimating experience

Our estimating team brings 20 years of hands-on experience across residential and commercial roofing. We understand how bids are built, where mistakes happen and what contractors need to move quickly from takeoff to pricing.

  • Roofing-specific measurement standards
  • Consistent waste factors and system logic
  • Clear deliverables designed for supplier pricing
  • Support for bid addenda and revisions

How we work

Real people. Real takeoffs.

Every SureSquare takeoff is completed by an experienced estimator, not auto-generated. Plans vary, scopes change and details matter. Human review makes sure your quantities reflect the drawings, the specs and real roofing conditions.

  • Every project reviewed by a qualified estimator
  • Details validated against plans and specifications
  • Quality checks completed before delivery
